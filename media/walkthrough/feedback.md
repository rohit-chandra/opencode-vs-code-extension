## Something broken or missing?

`/feedback` opens a short form. When you continue, a pre-filled issue opens on GitHub in your browser; you review it and press Submit there.

- Versions, your operating system and engine status can be attached, and you see exactly what before it leaves.
- Your prompts, files, paths and keys are never included.
- The extension has no telemetry. This form is the only way the maintainers hear from you.
